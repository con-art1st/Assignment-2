Before You Register

  • Every player must have an active FSU student ID and Campus Rec waiver on file.
  • Rosters lock after your team's second game — no additions after that.
  • A $25 forfeit deposit is refunded at season's end if your team plays every scheduled game.
  • Co-Rec division requires at least three players of each gender on the field at all times.
