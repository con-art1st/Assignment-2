About the League

Landis League started in 2011 as a single flag football bracket organized by a handful of students who wanted somewhere to play after intramural signups closed early. It's now run out of FSU Campus Recreation and fields more than eighty teams a semester across five sports, all played on and around Landis Green.

Every game is officiated by fellow students, every schedule is built around exam weeks, and every division — competitive, recreational, and co-rec — plays on the same fields under the same lights. You don't need experience to sign up. You need a team name and six friends willing to show up on time.

Champion Spotlight

The Landis Legends

Back-to-back basketball champions, the Legends went 14–0 across two seasons without a single forfeit — a league record. Captain Devon Ashe credits it to a group chat that never lets anyone forget game time.

Find the Fields

All Landis League games are played on the intramural fields and courts surrounding Landis Green, a short walk from the Student Services Building. Check the schedule for your specific field or court number before you head over.
