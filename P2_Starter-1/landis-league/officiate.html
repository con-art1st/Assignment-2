Officiate for the League

Game Officials

Officials run the games — calling fouls, tracking downs, keeping the clock, and making the judgment calls that keep a rec-league game from turning into an argument. Every sport pairs new officials with an experienced lead for their first two weeks, and Campus Rec runs a required one-hour rules clinic before the season starts.

No refereeing experience is required. Plenty of officials are former players who wanted to stay involved after their own team's season ended.

Scorekeepers & Site Supervisors

Scorekeepers track the game sheet and final score for basketball and dodgeball. Site supervisors oversee an entire night at one location — checking in officials, handling equipment, and resolving anything that comes up before it needs Campus Rec staff involved. Both roles are a good fit if you'd rather stay off the field but still want to be part of game night.

What Happens Next

  1. Campus Rec reviews applications on a rolling basis, usually within a week.
  2. You'll get an email with a rules clinic date for your sport.
  3. Attend the one-hour clinic and pick up your official's shirt.
  4. You'll be paired with an experienced official for your first two games.
  5. After that, you pick up shifts through the officiating sign-up sheet each week.
