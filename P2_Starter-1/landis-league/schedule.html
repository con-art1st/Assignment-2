League Glossary

Forfeit
A loss recorded automatically when a team can't field the minimum number of players ten minutes after game time.
Bye Week
A scheduled week off from games, used to keep an odd number of teams in a division balanced.
Seed
A team's ranking heading into the playoff bracket, based on regular-season record.
Sportsmanship Rating
A 1–5 score given by officials after every game; a team must average 3.0 or better to make the playoffs.
